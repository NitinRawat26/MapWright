When detection or mapping asks AI about a field no playbook recognises, its answer waits here. Approving adds the field name as a vocabulary term to a draft of the concept's domain playbook; the draft is then tested, reviewed and published like any other change. For a concept or attribute no playbook has yet, tick Create if missing: approving then drafts a new domain playbook, or adds the attribute to a draft of the concept's playbook. Rejecting changes nothing. A suggestion from a mapping's AI pass also approves or rejects that mapping row; clear its concept to approve only the row.
